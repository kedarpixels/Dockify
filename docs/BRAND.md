# Dockify brand

The mark is the whale itself: one flat Dockify blue whale facing left, with a rounded head, a small
eye cut out of the head, a long curved tail sweeping up into a two-lobe fluke, and three round spout
drops. There is no badge or background square behind it. Flat colour only: no gradients, glow,
shadows, outlines or texture.

## Colour

| Token | Hex | Use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Dockify blue | `#2396ED` | The whale, every surface and size |
| Eye | none | A real hole: it shows whatever sits behind the mark |

## Files

In `apps/web/public`.

| File | What it is |

| `../app/icon.png`, `../app/apple-icon.png` | Next file-convention favicons (512 transparent, 180 on white) |

## Rules

- The whale always faces left.
- Never put the whale inside a coloured square or circle.
- On dark surfaces the blue whale stays blue; the eye shows the dark page through it.
- Clear space around the mark: at least the height of the spout on every side.
